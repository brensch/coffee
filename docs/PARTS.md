## Removed

- **RV1 MOV (10D271K).** Behind the T500mA fuse a failing MOV can dissipate 60-110 W before the fuse opens; a real surge would blow the fuse anyway; the RECOM passes its 1 kV surge test unaided and every triac and opto-triac is an 800 V part. (Its footprint was also wrong: the 10D271K is a 12.5 mm disc on 7.5 mm pitch.)

## Assembly notes

- **Reflow order.** The ESP32-C6 module tolerates one reflow (datasheet section 12.3). Reflow the side without the module first; the module's side goes second. Through-hole parts are soldered last.
- **Copper.** 2 oz outer layers (docs/CIRCUIT.md): the heater current runs on both layers, 2-3 mm wide each, for ~15 mm.
